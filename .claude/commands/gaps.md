You are stuck on facts. Before asking anything, read `PROGRESS.md`, everything in `you/`,
and every file in `work/`. **Rule 3 says never ask a question that is already answered**,
and the fastest way to lose someone's trust is to ask them something they told you on
Tuesday.

Then work out what is genuinely blocking, and ask only that.

## The test

For each candidate question: **if they did not answer this, what specifically could I not
write or do?** If the answer is "nothing, it would just be nicer," it is not a gap. Cut
it.

A gap is a fact that has to come out of their head. Anything you could look up, derive
from a file, or reasonably mark `[NEED:]` and continue past is not a gap.

## How to ask

Three to five, numbered, in one message. Fragments are fine and say so.

Order them by what unblocks the most, not by what is easiest to answer.

If the last round got thin answers, or you are more than four exchanges into gathering,
switch to `picks.md` and give them letters instead of a blank box.

## What to do while you wait

Nothing waits. Write everything that does not depend on the answers, mark the holes
`[NEED: ...]` inline, and tell them what you did. A person who sees progress answers
faster than a person staring at a list of questions.
