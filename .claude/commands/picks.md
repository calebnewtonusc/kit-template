description: Multiple choice built from their own material. They answer with letters
---

The lowest-effort, highest-yield thing in the kit. Use it when they are tired, stuck,
facing a question about themselves they have never articulated, or when `/gaps` is
getting long.

Load the `interviewing-you` skill and write `PICKS.md`.

## The format

```markdown
## 14. A song that describes your life (180DC, one line)

- **A.** [option drawn from something they actually said]
- **B.** [genuinely different option, also from their material]
- **C.** [the funny one]
- **D.** None of these, here is mine: ___
```

## Rules

**Every option comes from something they actually told you.** Read `you/STORY-BANK.md`
and `you/PROFILE.md` first. You are giving them their own material back in a form
that takes four minutes to sort, not inventing a personality.

**Number continuously across the whole file**, so they can reply "14b, 15a, 16c, 17
none" and be done.

**Sub-letter compound questions.** For a prompt with parts, split it: 14a genre, 14b
who plays you, 14c the cliffhanger. Much easier to answer than one open question.

**Options must be genuinely different.** Three shades of the same answer is a wasted
question. If two options would produce nearly the same essay, cut one.

**One option should have teeth.** Funny, blunt, or slightly risky. Safe options across
the board produce a safe, generic answer, which is the failure mode this kit exists
to prevent.

**Always end with "none of these, here is mine."**

**Open with a one-line instruction:** "Reply with letters. 'A, C, B' is a complete
answer. Add 'none, here's mine' any time."

## After they answer

Write their picks into `you/STORY-BANK.md` so no future session asks again, then draft
against them. If a pick surprises you, ask one follow-up about that one only, then
proceed.
