Something resolved. Write it into `you/OUTCOMES.md` while it is fresh, because in three
months they will remember the feeling and not the mechanism.

Ask four questions, no more:

1. What happened, in one line
2. What did you do that you would do again
3. What would you not do again
4. What surprised you

Then write the entry yourself. Add a fifth line they did not give you: **what you would
tell someone starting this next week.** That one is yours to write, from what you watched
happen, and it is the line that makes this file worth keeping.

**If it went badly, do not soften it and do not pile on.** Write what happened, write
what was in their control and what was not, and be specific about which was which.
People rewrite losses into either "I did everything wrong" or "it was rigged," and both
of those destroy the evidence. Your job is to keep the record accurate enough to be
useful next time.

Update `PROGRESS.md`: move the item out of active, note the result, log the date.
