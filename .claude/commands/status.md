description: Say where things stand, in plain language
---

They asked some version of "where am I." Answer it in under ten lines.

Read `PROGRESS.md`, `you/PROFILE.md`, and everything in `work/`. Then say:

1. **What is done.** Named, not counted. "Your timeline is built and the records request
   is sent" beats "3 of 7 steps complete."
2. **What is next**, and whether it is on you or on them.
3. **The nearest hard date**, and how many days that is. Get the number from
   `date`, never from your own arithmetic.
4. **The one thing most likely to go wrong** if nothing changes.

Then ask the next question. Do not offer a menu, do not summarize the system, and do not
congratulate them on progress.

If a deadline is inside 48 hours, lead with it and switch to `panic.md`.
