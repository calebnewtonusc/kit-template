# The kit list

Every idea, scored against the seven-property test in [MAKING-A-KIT.md](MAKING-A-KIT.md).
Score is out of 7. **Five or more is kit-shaped. Four or fewer should stay a skill.**

The properties: (1) spans sessions, (2) bottleneck is extraction not generation, (3) a
hidden evaluator, (4) mechanical constraint a model fails, (5) rare and high-stakes,
(6) facts must stay consistent across artifacts, (7) a professional would run a process
most people do not know exists.

## Deliberately not kits

Named here so nobody builds them twice. All real value, all the wrong container: a
single `SKILL.md` does the job in an hour.

Cover letter writing. Reading one paper. Interview practice for one interview. Meal
planning. Workout programming. Anything that finishes in one sitting and produces no
artifact anybody else will read.
