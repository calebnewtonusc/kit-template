## The dating rule

**Every fact here carries the date it was last confirmed.** Not the date it was true,
the date somebody checked.

A fact does not have to be false to cause damage, it only has to have expired. Anything
describing your current situation moves without telling you. `tools/stale.sh` finds
them, `/refresh` fixes them, and anything unchecked for three weeks gets read back
before it matters.
